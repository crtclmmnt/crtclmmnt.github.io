Cutting Through
Political Materialism


Welcome to my blog. I'm calling it Cutting Through Political Materialism in reference to a great book by Chögyam Trungpa Rinpoche. In his book Cutting Through Spiritual Materialism, he describes the ego, what it is, and how it uses spirituality in service of itself when the spiritual practitioner is not careful.

I think something similar happens with political ideologies. I think when we're not careful, our desire for self-importance can motivate us to use politics as an excuse to reinforce values, beliefs, and feelings that have no relevance to a liberatory movement.

So I will be writing about radical politics, contemplative practice, ecological identity, and what it means to let politics change you. Most importantly, this is a blog about things I find interesting. If you would like to recieve new posts in your inbox, please sign up for my newsletter here.
